Online (`net`, the "Online play" block before Boot):
- Roles are `off`, `host` and `client`. The host runs everything (`update()` is unchanged) and streams `netSnapshot()` 20 times a second (compact arrays: `sh` sheep, `en` enemies, `p` players with `NET_PF`, `h` HUD numbers) over an unreliable channel; events (sounds, text, shake, particles, splats) are recorded by wrappers (`netHookEffects`) and sent on the reliable channel.
- Guests build the world from a `world` message (`netClientWorld`, same seeds, `net.forceSeed` for the daily route), skip the simulation (`netClientUpdate`: apply the snapshot, then dead-reckon) and send `in` (move vector, 30 Hz) and `act` (action or ability).
- Remote players on the host have `dev` `net<id>` and `p.net`; `readMove` and `netRemoteEvent` serve them. `isMine(p)` picks whose camera and attack meter to use. `skinOk` is permissive online so a guest's unlocked breed is used.
- Signalling is manual: `netHostInvite`, `netGuestAccept`, `netHostAccept`, with codes packed by `netPack` (deflate + base64url, prefix `GEB1.`). The copy/paste box is HTML (`#netbox`, `netOpenBox`).
- States `NET` (menu), `NETWAIT` (guest lobby); screens `drawNet`, `drawNetWait`, `drawNetOverlay`. `NET_VER` must match between players.

Demo: `DEMO`, `DEMO_MAPS`, `DEMO_WAVES`, `DEMO_BREEDS`, `DEMO_LOCKED_MODES`; the `DEMOEND` screen is `demoEnd(reason)`. `tools/build_demo.sh` flips the flag and renames save keys.

## Snapshot
- The whole game is in `index.html`. Its sections are: canvases, palette and remap, sprites, font, audio, world generation and rendering, camera, input devices, state, particles, collision, players, herd boids, enemies, modes, lobby, update, render, and boot.
- **Resolution:** `resize()` picks an integer pixel scale `PIX` and sizes the buffer `W×H` (W 120–560, H 180–420) to fill the screen. The world buffer has a 1px margin for sub-pixel camera presentation. The HUD is a separate canvas that is never palette-swapped. All layout must use `W`/`H`, never fixed numbers.
- **Screens:** `TITLE → MODE → PLAYERS → PLAYING → RESULTS / GAMEOVER`, plus `OPTIONS` and `HOWTO`. List screens are built by `screenItems()` and handled by `listEvent()`. The player-select screen uses `playersEvent()`. Taps hit-test against `hits`, which is rebuilt every frame.
- **Rusty Lab animals (v0.10):**
  - `LAB` holds the lab data: `units` (goat, wolf, fox, the ten farm dogs; compact `R` row table plus `F` frame indices and `O` offsets), `items` (hair, hat, outfit, acc), `sets` (item colour options) and `fixed` colours. Boss, bat and hawk are added at boot with `labFixedUnit()`.
  - Frames are built by `labSet(unitId, look)` (cached): `labCompose()` is a port of the lab's compose (under-accessories, base, outfit, hair, hat, accessories), then `labPose()` for the derived poses, then `labScale()` (2/3, detail-preserving), then `labColors()` and `labCanvas()`.
  - The resulting set looks like the old one: `set[dir]` holds the walk frames, plus `run`, `idle`, `jump`, `attack`, `act` and `pose.{sit, lie, alert, sniff, bite}`. dir is E, SE, NE, S or N, and each `LabSpr` carries the drawn west-facing art in `.l`.
  - A look is `{coat, eyes, hair, hat, outfit, acc[], col{hair, hat, outfit, acc}}`, sanitized by `normLabLook()`. `skinSet(kind, breed, look)` resolves breed and look into a set. For goats the breed sets the coat, and `DOG_BREEDS[].unit` names the lab unit.
  - Off-palette lab colours are registered in `LAB_EXTRA` and remapped in `buildRemap()` via `labRemapInto()`.
  - STYLE screen: `openStyle(p)`, `styleItems()` and `drawStyle()`. Looks are saved in `settings.looks[kind]`; `cpuLook()` styles the CPU helpers.
- **Animal sprites (v0.9, replaced in v0.10; facing and gait logic still apply):**
  - Goats come from the region templates `GT` and `GLEGS` (letters h horn, f head, K eye, e ear, m muzzle, z beard, c coat, d shade, L leg, k hoof). `goatColor()` maps the letters per entry of `GOAT_BREEDS`, applying spots, face stripes and a dorsal stripe, and `goatSet(breed)` builds the set.
  - Dogs, wolves and foxes come from `canineSet(look)`, using `canineSide` for the E, SE and NE views and `canineFront` for S and N. A look has the body parameters L, B, Lg, Hd, muz, ears, tail and fluffy, plus colour keys and a pattern.
  - Every set has `[dir][4 walk frames]`, `run[dir][4]` (each frame carries `.bob`) and `act[dir]`. Canines also have `pose.{sit, lie, alert, sniff[4], bite}`.
  - `faceDir8()` is used for all players, wolves and foxes. Sheep still use the 6-direction `faceDir()`.
  - `stepGait(o, dt, on, off)` advances `o.stepPh` (shared by walk and gallop) and switches `o.gait` with hysteresis. `faceDirH(o)` and `faceLeft(o)` give stable facing. `actorSprite()` and `drawEnemy()` read these, never `p.walk` or time.
  - Great Wolf: `BOSS_T` templates (E, S, N) plus `bossLegs()` and `outlineAll()` build `SP.boss`, drawn at 1× (not scaled).
- **Modes:** `escort`, `defend`, `daily`, `endless`, `versus` and `custom` (an editor map). `isEscort()` covers every escort-like mode. `escortWorld(src)` builds the world for a map index, `'daily'`, `'endless'` or `'custom'`.
- **Mask values:** 0 meadow, 1 forest, 2 pillar, 3 ford (slow), 4 deep water (blocked), 5 brambles (slow; tangles sheep). `blocked()` and `terrainSlow()` read these. Hazards come from `addHazards(def)`, using `def.rivers` and `def.brambles`.
- **Weather:** `def.weather` is `rain`, `wind`, `fog` or `storm`. `applyRemap()` also does the fog and night dithering using a vision grid built by `buildVisGrid()`.
- **Creatures:** enemy types are wolf (with alpha and boss variants), bat, fox and hawk. Sheep types are normal, ram, lamb and golden. Player kinds are goat, dog and wolf (Versus only).
- **Progression:** `bells` and `upg` reset each run. `progress.medals` and `progress.daily` are saved in `goatGuardianMaps`. `GOAT_SKINS` and `DOG_SKINS` alias `GOAT_BREEDS` and `DOG_BREEDS`. `p.skin` is the breed index, unlocked by `totalMedals()` against `need`; during a showcase every breed is allowed (`skinOk`). `skinSet()` caches the generated sets. The last breed picked is saved as `settings.goatBreed` and `settings.dogBreed`.
- **Dog skills:** `dogBreed(p)` gives the stats (`speed`, `push`, `ability`, `cd`, plus the passives `barkStun`, `ratter`, `bellwether`, `hide` and `aura`). `dogAbility()` dispatches the skill. Dashes use `p.dashT`, `p.dashKind` and `dashHits()`; the pounce ends in `ambushBlast()`. Sprint uses `p.sprintT` and the frenzy uses `p.frenzyN`, which calls `barkBlast()`. A Bernese carry sets `p.carry` (the sheep leaves the `sheep` array, and win and lose checks count it), and `dropSheep()` puts it back. `knockOut()` and `biteEnemy()` are the shared take-down helpers. CPU skill use is in `aiDogSkill()`.
- **Editor:** the `ED` object holds a 75x175 grid of 8px cells. Share codes look like `GG1|rle|pillars|start|goal` and are saved under `goatGuardianCustom`.
- **Birds:** the `birds` array is filled by `spawnFlock(big)` and updated by `updateBirds()`. `drawBirdShadows()` draws the shadows on the ground layer and `drawBirds()` draws the birds above everything.
- **Trailer:** the `TRAILER` scene list is played through the showcase system with `showcase.trailer = true`. In trailer mode `resize()` uses `dh / 180`, which gives a closer camera, and `drawTrailerOverlay()` draws the cards, letterbox and captions. `window.__trailerMark(id)` is called at every cut so `tools/trailer/` can time the soundtrack.
- **Music:** `updateMusic()` schedules the `THEMES` loops on `musicBus` every frame.
- **PWA:** the service worker registers only when the page is served over http(s), has the manifest link, and isn't running inside an artifact.
- **Pause menu:** while `paused` is true, `pauseView` selects the sub-view: main, options, controls, restart or exit. `pauseItems()` builds each list and `openPause()` opens the menu. The title Exit goes to the `BYE` menu state.
- **Maps:** `ESCORT_MAPS` and `DEFEND_MAPS` each hold 10 definitions. Map 0 in each is the legacy generator (`makeEscortWorld(2, 7777)` and `makeDefendWorld(4242)`). The other maps use `makeEscortMap(def, seed)` and `makeDefendMap(def, seed)` with fixed seeds. `getMapWorld()` keeps a cache of up to 5 maps and `mapPreview()` draws the minimaps. Best results are stored in `localStorage` under `goatGuardianMaps`. Loading goes through `requestLoad()` and the `LOADING` state.
- **CPU helpers:** players with `cpu: true` and `dev` set to `ai<slot>`. `joinCPU()` and `removeCPU()` add and remove them, and `updateAI()` drives them whenever `hasCPU()` is true. Auto helpers are flagged `auto` and removed when you return to Player Select.
- **Demo:** `showcase` and `SCENES` script the attract-mode demo. `updateAI()` drives players whose `dev` is `ai0`–`ai3` through `aiVec`. `startShowcase()` saves the real players and `endShowcase()` restores them. While the demo runs, `rngFn` is a seeded mulberry32 generator.
- **Input:** `dispatch()` runs synchronously inside DOM handlers so fullscreen requests count as user gestures. Gamepads are polled each frame.
- **Palette:** all world art must use the exact `PAL_DAY` hex colours. `applyRemap()` swaps them each frame toward the sunset and dusk palettes. Any colour outside the palette is not swapped, so avoid alpha blending and rotation in world drawing.
- **World mask:** 0 is meadow, 1 is forest, 2 is a pillar. Players and sheep collide with forest and pillars. Wolves pass through the forest and are drawn only as eyes while inside it.
- **Escort:** each mission builds a new world from a path that starts at the bottom and ends at the top. Sheep inside the goal ring are `penned` and safe.
- **Input:** devices are `kbL`, `kbR`, `touch`, and `pad0`–`pad3`. They produce events, which the lobby or the game consumes.
- **Saved data:** best scores are stored in `localStorage` under `goatGuardianBest2` as `{escort, defend}`. Settings are stored under `goatGuardianSettings` as `{sound, shake, fullscreen}`.
- **Assets:** no Universal Assets are used. Everything is procedural.

Chunk Lab: Ink Outlaws

v0.5.0 · hand-drawn ink style · JavaScript painter

WASD/arrows move · Shift run · Space jump · J/click attack · Q/E or 1–5 weapon · G dance. With fists, a single press is the big swing; press again within 0.4 s to chain left hit, right hit and the finisher. Guns fire from the muzzle anchor of each frame.

Tap a direction to select it; the centre shows it large. All eight directions are projected from one fixed-length 3D skeleton. Left and right are drawn at the three-quarter angle.

# Unit roster

Every lab preset (63) is used. New looks are built with the lab schema in `src/config/units.js`, where they can be pasted into the
lab's customizer and back.

| Faction | Units |
|---|---|
| Outlaws: starting captains | Clay Harrow (revolver), Goro the Porter (katana), Wren Tolliver (rifle), The Stranger (your lab design) |
| Outlaws: for hire | Kit, Pike, Paco, Dunn, Tully, Fizz, Pip, Hob, Big Abel, Juniper, Vane, Reyes, Nori, Mo, Pell, Finch, Vega, Ulf, Brannoc, Jory, Vesper, Pops Vinyl, Captain Morrow |
| Outlaws: prisoners | Holloway, El Trueno, Hazel, Maren, Aldous, Wade, Big Tobias, Bramble (on Moss), Ezra (on Clementine) |
| Outlaws: other | Biscuit the cat (camp pet), Ash, Clementine, Moss, Gila (mounts), Town Militia*, Town Rifle* |
| Greenskin Corsairs | Grub, Goblin Slinger*, Powder Monkey*, Bog Shaman*, Goblin Bruiser*, Lizard Rider*, Snap, Bog Pup*, Giant Lizard (Gila), Gloop and Slimelings, Ember Slime*, Chomp (mimic), Grizzle the Imp (elite), **Captain Snig** (boss; on a lizard* at half health) |
| Hollow Court | Bone Guard*, Bone Rifle*, Reaper*, Lantern Wisp*, Wick, Grave Hound*, Hex Cat*, Old Crow, The Wrapped Man, The Brothers Grim, Fang Ronin, Mortimer Bone, Old Hagga (summoner), Gallows Oak, Ash Oak*, **Queen Vex** (boss) |
| Gilded Crown | Footman*, Crossbow*, Halberdier*, Musketeer*, Knight Lancer*, Cleric* (healer), Sir Rowan, Lord Aldric, Kestor Ironhoof, Brasswood, Bulwark, Rattle, Tyrant (rex), **King Odo** (boss; on horseback* at half health) |
